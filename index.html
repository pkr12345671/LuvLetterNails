Before you book

Policies & questions

How do I book an appointment?

First, DM me on Instagram for a consultation — we’ll go over your design and I’ll tell you exactly what to select. Then book in the Book section and pay your $20 deposit. Appointments booked without a DM consultation are cancelled & refunded, and no slot is reserved until the deposit is paid. For a quick booking, send your design/inspo pic, desired date & time, and whether you need a removal.

What kind of designs do you do?

I specialize in maximalist, six+ swatch designs with lots of color, texture, and sculpted & hand-drawn elements. One-color / simple sets are currently offered to regular clients only. Not sure if your design fits? Check my IG feed and inspo highlights to compare.

Rescheduling, cancellations & fees

Please notify me at least 24 hours before your appointment through DM to reschedule or cancel. You may reschedule twice with the same deposit; after that you’ll need to cancel and rebook. Last-minute reschedules are charged 25% of the service price, last-minute cancellations 50%.

Late arrivals & no-shows

There’s a 15-minute grace period. After 15 minutes with no communication, a $10 late fee applies. After 30 minutes with no communication it’s a no-show, charged 100% of the service price (and you may be banned from booking). Appointments starting at 8 PM or later are late-night appointments and include a $15 late-night fee.

Sickness, masks & guests

If you have any symptoms of sickness — or have been around someone who’s sick — please don’t come in; message me and we’ll reschedule with no charge. Showing up sick means being turned away and charged the cancellation fee. Masks are optional but highly recommended (I have disposables if you need one). Please don’t bring additional guests unless they’re booked for a service or I’ve agreed beforehand.

Are the press-ons reusable?

Yes — built with sturdy builder-gel layers so, with proper care and the right adhesive, they can be worn again and again. Not sure of your sizes? Add Press-Ons Sizing when you book.
