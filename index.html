Care & questions

How do the press-ons fit?

Each set is made to order and sized to you. If you don’t know your sizes, request a sizing kit on the form and we’ll get you measured before we make your set.

Are the press-ons reusable?

Yes — built with sturdy builder-gel layers so, with proper care and the right adhesive, they can be worn again and again.

How long does shipping take?

Sets are hand-crafted to order, so production time plus shipping applies. We’ll confirm the current turnaround when we reply to your request. (Final timelines confirmed by the artist.)
